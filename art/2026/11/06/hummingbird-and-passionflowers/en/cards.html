Loud flowers.
A quiet bird.

Daily Art / 2026.11.0602 / 04

Give the eye a circuit

COMPOSITION

Red blooms set an uneven route.
A white breast pauses it; mist opens the left.

COLOUR

Muted green meets saturated red.
The tiny bird stands out through value.

LIGHT

Soft distance. Clearer foreground.
A pale haze carries a brighter breast.

Detail, then dissolution

MEDIUM · FACT

Oil on canvas, c. 1875–1885.
Fine detail meets soft haze: a visual reading.

ARTIST · FACT

Martin Johnson Heade · 1819–1904.
American; painting hummingbirds by 1862.

RESPONSE · OPINION

Flowers open. Vines turn. Wings stay folded.
The smallest body slows the whole forest.
