Let the edges hold the light

COMPOSITION

Canopy at the centre. Bodies at the base.
Upper-left moon ↔ lower-right grain stack.

COLOUR

Ochre and rust: warm analogous hues.
Muted green steadies them. White stays rare.

LIGHT

Dark trees lift the grain; sleeves reveal bodies.
Brightness becomes a scattered rhythm.
