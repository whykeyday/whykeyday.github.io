Daily Art / 2026.10.2102 / 04

Cool colors gather. Warm colors spark.

COMPOSITION

A narrow mouth gathers the stems.
The bouquet opens unevenly above.

COLOR

Neighboring blues, greens and violets;
small red-blue near-complementary sparks.

LIGHT

Pale flowers interrupt dark leaves.
Value rhythm keeps complexity legible.

Daily Art / 2026.10.2103 / 04

The grain of paper. The breath of flowers.

TECHNIQUE / FACT

Pastel on paper. Visible grain,
short touches and fine stems.

ARTIST / FACT

Odilon Redon, French, 1840–1916.
Interested in biology from a young age.

RESPONSE / VIEW

Reaching, turning, drooping.
One quiet vase holds different gestures.
