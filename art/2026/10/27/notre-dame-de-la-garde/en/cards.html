Notre-Dame de la Garde (La Bonne Mère), Marseille

A Harbour, Made of Light

Masts measure the sky. Small strokes return colour to the water.

Daily Art / 2026.10.2702 / 04

Density at the edges. Room for light.

COMPOSITION

Masts crowd the sides. A pale channel lets the eye pass through.

COLOUR THEORY

Local blue–orange and violet–yellow contrasts. Neighbouring hues soften the joins.

LIGHT

Dark hulls anchor; pale hills recede. Horizontal marks loosen the reflections.

Let distance finish the picture.

TECHNIQUE / FACT

Separate rectangular strokes, like tesserae. Pigment up close; harbour at a distance. [1]

ARTIST / FACT

French painter, 1863–1935. A major Neo-Impressionist. [2]

RESPONSE / VIEW

Density leaves a channel open. Even a full world offers somewhere to rest.
