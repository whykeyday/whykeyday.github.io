## 2. China holds LPR for a 16th month as policies diverge

Rates / FX | September 20 release; Fed context from September 16

**Facts / attributed claims: ** Reuters reports that China kept its one-year LPR at 3.00% and its five-year LPR at 3.50%, unchanged for a 16th month and matching all 21 survey responses. Separately, the Fed’s September 16 statement confirms a 25-basis-point increase to 3.75%–4.00%. LPR is a lending benchmark, not a like-for-like equivalent of the federal funds rate.

**Market impact (inference): ** Unchanged loan quotes offer no new broad lending-rate relief. Policy divergence remains relevant to cross-border financing and yuan assets, but rate differences alone cannot determine the currency’s direction.

**Countercase and risks (scenarios): ** A steady LPR does not rule out targeted support. Credit demand, fiscal measures, trade negotiations and capital flows could change the market response.
