01New Zealand holds by a 4–3 vote; the kiwi falls

Decision released October 28; FX figures are a Reuters intraday snapshot.

Facts: The RBNZ held its official cash rate at 2.75%; three of seven members preferred 3%. Reuters reported an intraday NZD/USD low of 0.55831, down as much as 1.7%. The primary decision was checked against Reuters and AP; syndication hosts are not additional sources.

Market impact | inference: The surprise reduces near-term interest-rate support for the currency. It may ease local borrowing pressure while increasing import costs. This is a transmission inference, not a prediction for every maturity.

Counter-risk: The central bank still sees a likely need for tighter policy, conditional on the outlook. Persistent inflation could reverse the initial currency move; a pause does not establish an easing cycle.

02Australian inflation rises: keep monthly and quarterly measures separate

Released October 28; reference periods are September and the third quarter.

Facts: ABS reported September monthly CPI inflation of 4.3% year on year, versus 4.0% in August, and monthly trimmed-mean inflation of 3.6%. Quarterly trimmed-mean inflation was about 1.0% quarter on quarter and 3.7% year on year, cross-checked with bank research and independent financial reporting. The 3.6% and 3.7% readings are different series.

Market impact | inference: Persistent underlying inflation strengthens the case for a hike next week, supporting rate differentials but pressuring consumption and property finance. That is an inference; the bank’s hike forecast is an opinion.

Counter-risk: Energy and expiring subsidies affect prices, while housing inflation is moderating. Weaker demand could justify waiting. One release does not determine the decision.

05Oil rises after a refinery attack; inventories still await verification

Attack reporting: October 27–28. Price snapshot: October 28, 08:58 UTC.

Facts: Reuters and AP separately reported a Saudi refinery attack and supply concerns. At 08:58 UTC Reuters quoted Brent futures at $115.24 a barrel, up 1.2%, and WTI at $101.52, up 1.6%. EIA’s schedule indicates the usual Wednesday release at 10:30 EDT. Today’s inventory outcome was still pending at cutoff.

Market impact | inference: Refining disruption may raise fuel and transport costs before feeding into margins and inflation expectations. Crude gains alone do not quantify a physical shortage.

Counter-risk: Repairs, alternative supply or weaker demand could reduce the premium. Contract months, timestamps, crude and refined products must remain distinct. Production, exports and inventories need confirmation.

06Payments earnings: separate revenue growth from profit adjustments

Released after the US close on October 27; reviewed for today’s morning edition.

Facts: The payments network with ticker V reported fiscal fourth-quarter net revenue of about $12.1 billion, up 13%, in its regulatory filing. GAAP EPS was $3.19; adjusted EPS was $3.48. Reuters reporting and MarketBeat earnings data provide cross-checks. A regulatory copy of company material counts as one originating source.

Market impact | inference: Growth offers company-level evidence of resilient spending and cross-border activity. Sustained transactions could support infrastructure revenue, but nominal spending includes inflation and does not measure real consumption growth.

Counter-risk: Adjusted profit exceeds GAAP profit, requiring scrutiny of exclusions and recurring costs. One global network does not represent every household. Incentives, competition and regulation may constrain margins.
