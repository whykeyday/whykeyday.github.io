*MACRO / CONSUMPTION*

An earlier industry survey showed September sales value rising 1.3% year on year; that measure is not directly comparable with official volumes.

**Countercase and risks:** Warm weather may have delayed clothing purchases. Quarterly growth and upward revisions to earlier months caution against treating one monthly fall as evidence of recession.

## 02 · Japan inflation: above target, with the policy decision still ahead

*RATES / CURRENCIES*

**Facts:** September core CPI rose 2.1% year on year, according to releases reported on 23 October in Japan. This measure excludes fresh food but includes energy. Reuters and AFP separately reported the reading. The next Bank of Japan meeting is scheduled for 29–30 October; reports of a possible hold are expectations, not a decision.

**Market implications | inference:** An above-target reading strengthens the case for further tightening and may affect Japanese bond yields and yen interest-rate differentials. Energy inflation, however, has different policy implications from persistent wage and service-price pressure.

**Countercase and risks:** Weaker demand or subsidies could ease inflation. Safe-haven flows and overseas rates also drive the yen, so neither an immediate hike nor sustained appreciation follows automatically.
