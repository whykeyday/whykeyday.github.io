Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: panoramic editorial image for Telotia
corporate landing page, 1536x1024 landscape composition suitable for a wide crop.
Create a restrained contemporary Chinese ink-wash artwork on very warm ivory
xuan paper (#F8F6F2) with visible soft paper fibres. A single fine muted teal
thread travels horizontally across a quiet ink-wash landscape of two distant
abstract stone/paper formations. One small slender form on the left, a small
layered group on the right, connected delicately by the same thread crossing
negative space. Suggest people connecting with organizations through composition
only, no literal network icons or diagrams. Generous blank upper half; ink
concentrates in lower third. Real ink diffusion, nuanced grey/blue-green washes,
subtle shadows. Thoughtful, warm, calm, premium editorial, sparse asymmetry.
No text, no logo, no letters, no frames, no filled neon shapes, no medical symbols,
no digital UI, no glowing sci-fi effects.

Prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial supporting image for
Telotia company website. Landscape 1536x1024, overhead close-up of a quiet
collaborative worktable in soft natural daylight. Three adult hands, from
different people, collaborating over blank ivory paper and a simple unmarked

placing an unmarked paper shape, a third resting naturally. Authentic tactile
paper fibres, linen sleeve, restrained warm off-white and graphite palette with
one subtle muted teal detail. Thoughtful candid composition, art-directed but
believable, plenty of breathing room, no faces. This is a conceptual editorial
illustration of collaboration, not a real team portrait. No text, no letters,
no logos, no business charts, no screens, no medical equipment, no handshake
